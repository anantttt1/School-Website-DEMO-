Welcome to AP School, Mathura

Established with a vision to redefine quality education in the heart of Mathura, AP School stands as a beacon of academic rigor, moral integrity and all-round development. Our sprawling campus, experienced faculty and progressive pedagogy create an environment where every child discovers their potential.

We believe education is not merely the acquisition of knowledge but the cultivation of character, curiosity and courage. Our students leave as confident, compassionate and capable individuals ready to lead.
